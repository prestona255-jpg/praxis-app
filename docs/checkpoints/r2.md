## Departures from the drawing or the prompt (named, with the reason)
1. **The panel's place on a phone (B2).** The prompt: the panel's top 8 px under the top of the page. Built: 8 px under the
   Shelf's compact bar (68 px from the top, measured). Reason: the first capture showed the compact bar (position:fixed,
   60 px, on once the head scrolls away) lying over the panel's heading and Done — Done could not be tapped. The drawing does
   not draw that bar. From 760 the panel sits 8 px under the app's top bar (88 px, measured). An empty shelf does not scroll.
2. **B9's "function named in 0.5".** There is none: the book page writes inline. r2SetStatus repeats its statements.
3. **Inline styles the drawing's own markup carries**: waitHtml's `align-self:center;gap:10px;width:100%` on the placeholder
   rows, handForm's `margin-top:0` on its buttons, and the panel's results holder `display:contents` (the drawing's body()).
4. **An edition with no cover of its own** is saved with no cover when it has an ISBN (scanCommitBook then asks by that
   ISBN), and with the work's cover when it has neither.
5. **A by-hand book already on the shelf** (same title and author) is not saved again; it is listed under the scan rows as
   its owned row (B13 says "folded is handled as in B6").
6. **After the first book on an empty shelf** the panel stays open and becomes the ordinary panel ("Find a book", Done).
7. **Cover hosts accepted**: https on covers.openlibrary.org, books.google.com, books.googleusercontent.com — the hosts
   openLibraryIsbnCover and googleBooksLargestCover build. A data: or http: cover, or any other host, draws the tile.
8. **The panel and Search have separate visits**: a book added on Search's All books side is listed there, not in the panel,
   and Undo belongs to the room that added it.
9. **Undo ends when the reader leaves the room** the book was added in (the red-team's F1). B7 says Undo is "only for an id
   in this visit's created list", and B11 keeps the visit through a book page. Built narrower: leaving the Shelf (or Search)
   empties the created list, so after Back the book is an owned row with its status and no Undo. Reason: a book opened from
   the panel's own list and given notes or an artifact, then Undone on the way back, would lose those with it (deleteBook
   scrubs the artifact, notes' links, arcs and lenses). Preston may strike this and keep Undo for the whole visit.
10. **Back to the panel redraws its list from memory** (B11): the same words and rows, with what was added shown as owned.

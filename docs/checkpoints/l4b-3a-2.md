## The look, as built

- A section is a shelf: its name at the left in the display face (a lens shows its mark before the name), "See all N" with a
  chevron at the right; under it a row of covers 168 px high, each at its own image's width, standing on one slim plain ledge
  that runs the width of the row with a soft shade under it; under the ledge each book's title (two lines at most) and author.
- The ground is one flat warm colour to every edge of the screen; no card behind the Shelf.
- The head: "Shelf", the three dots, Add a book; the count line; then ONE row holding the Lenses | Categories switch and a search
  button; then the status chips. The search field is not on show until the button is tapped; it then takes the switch row's place.
- On phones, once the head has scrolled away, a compact bar is fixed at the top: "Shelf", the switch, the search button.
- Groups that hold one book are not given a shelf of their own; they are listed in a card at the end of the page ("More shelves"
  in Categories, "More lenses" in Lenses), one row each.

Preston approved a preview on 21 Nov and has seen captures of this patch; he has not seen it on his phone — that check is owed
after the push.

## Preston's rulings (Shelf chat, 21 Nov 2026), as worded

- **A1** Row length: "First 12, then See all". A row holds the first twelve books of its group (fewer where fewer fit on a
  desktop); "See all N" opens the group's own page. This replaces S4 of L4b-3a ("Row on phone, wrap on desktop").
- **A2** The header: "The whole header opens it". Anywhere on a section's header opens the lens page or the category page.
- **A3** Lenses: "Mark beside the name; one-book lenses listed".
- **A4** The cover with no image: "Keep both". The title and author stay on the card, and also sit under the ledge.
- **A5** Search: "Field replaces the switch row". X or Escape clears the search and closes the field. The compact bar's search
  button goes to the top of the page first.
- **A6** Desktop: "One shelf, as many as fit". One row per section, no wrapping, no sideways scroll.
- **A7** The small-shelf rule: "One book only". A group is small when it holds one book in all, whatever a chip is hiding.
- **A8** Colours: "Mix them from existing colours". No new tokens; assets/theme.css is not touched.
- **A9** Other states: "Yes, as shown". Select mode shows every book and no "More" list; the pile (books with no category) shows
  every book and has no "See all"; the Yumi lens row, the first-shelf card and the empty shelf are unchanged.
- **A10** The Yumi flower in Select mode (red-team finding 5 of L4b-3a.1, held there as R-5): "Commit as built, narrow it
  later". This build is the later: on a phone the flower is hidden only while the Select bar is showing (a book is picked) or the
  Manage sheet is open, and is back in Select mode with nothing picked. **This closes L4b-3a.1's R-5.**
- **A11** The door's words under a chip or a search: "6 of 12". When a filter leaves fewer books than the group holds, the header
  says "N of M" (N matching, M in the group, which is what the door opens); otherwise "See all N".
- **A12** A library where every category holds one book: "One shared shelf". In Categories they stand together on one shelf
  named "Your books", each book with its category under it in place of the author. Shown a capture of both.

## Choices the Shelf chat made — each Preston's to overrule

- **C1** The compact bar's switch buttons are 44 px high (the preview drew 40). 44 is the app's floor for a tap target.
- **C2** A cover's width follows its image: round(168 × width / height), held between 96 and 168 px. An image wider than square
  is cropped at the sides (object-fit: cover). Until the image loads the slot is 112 px (2:3); it then settles once.
- **C3** The words under a cover keep at least 108 px, so a 12-letter word ("Undercommons", 104 px) is not split. A book with no
  cover image has a 120 px card (not 112), so the same word stays whole on the card too. A longer word can still break.
- **C4** Desktop: the cap is as many 2:3 covers as fit the row (7 at 1360, 5 at 820 to 900, 4 at 760). Covers wider than 2:3
  that would pass the row's content edge (its right padding kept clear) are not shown and stay behind "See all N" (sh3TrimRow).
- **C5** A shelf showing one book says "See it", not "See all 1".
- **C6** A group with one book keeps its own shelf when (a) it is a lens and every lens holds one book (the shared shelf of A12 is
  Categories only), (b) it is the only group, or (c) it is a category whose name the category page would refuse. Under a chip
  the rule still reads the whole group (A7). The shared shelf has no page of its own: its header is still ("N books"), it shows
  every book, and from 760 it wraps like the pile.
- **C7** Select mode, the pile, the shared shelf and a search on a desktop wrap onto more rows, each book standing on its own
  piece of ledge. A search shows every match on phones too (the row swipes). A chip alone keeps the cap of twelve.
- **C8** The compact bar's switch re-renders the Shelf, which lands at the top with the head's switch focused.
- **C9** New words: "See all N", "See it", "N of M", "Your books", "More shelves", "More lenses", "1 book · <title>", "N books"
  on a header that is not a door, the aria-labels "Open the lens X, N books" / "Open the category X, N books" (with " shown, of
  M" under A11), "Your books, one from each category", "Close search". The placeholder and the no-match line are L4b-3a's.
- **C10** The ground and the ledge are colour-mix() of existing tokens (the stylesheet already uses colour-mix 137 times).
  Computed in the chat's Chrome: Night ground rgb(36,24,15), ledge rgb(90,71,51); Day ground rgb(252,250,245), ledge
  rgb(219,211,198). **This rig computed exactly these.**
- **C11** The picked ring (3 px, --gold) and the keyboard focus ring (2 px, --gold-ink, with a 2 px line of --page inside it so it
  shows on any cover art) sit inside the cover's edge. L4b-3a drew them 2 px outside.
- **C12** An image under 24 px on either side is treated as a cover that failed: the next candidate is tried, else the title card
  stands and the book is marked in coverBrokenIds as any failed cover is.

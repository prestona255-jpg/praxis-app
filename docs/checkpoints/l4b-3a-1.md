## Choices the chat made — each Preston's to overrule

- **F1** P1's cause as the chat reads it: `revealShelfPanel` places a panel 8 px below the very top of the screen, which on a phone
  with a notch is under the status bar. Normally the fixed band `#statusbar-scrim` covers that strip. With the keyboard up iOS lifts
  every fixed layer (his screenshot shows the tab bar mid-screen), the band leaves, and the Title field is behind the clock. The fix
  leaves room equal to the band's own height, which is the top safe area on a notched phone and 0 everywhere else. **Reasoned from
  the screenshot, not observed:** no rig lifts fixed layers the way iOS does.
- **F2** P2: on phones the closed Manage sheet and the closed Select bar are `visibility:hidden`. The visibility change waits out
  the slide, so each still closes as it did.
- **F3** P3: on phones the Yumi flower is hidden while the Manage sheet is open AND while Select mode is on (it also sat beside
  Cancel). With it out of the way the Select bar is centred again with 22 px at the right, where L4b-3a had left 92 px for the
  flower; "2 selected", Move and Cancel then share one row at 390.
- **F4** P4: Move and Cancel get `min-height:44px` at every width; Cancel also `min-width:44px` and 12 px side padding.

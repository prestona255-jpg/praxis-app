## §1 — FINDING

The arc interior has the pre-WALL shelf disease: cream panels floating on a dark ground, seven mixed-dialect controls scattered top-right, voids between rows, empty panels standing as furniture, and the answer to the arc's central question — the most meaning-dense words in the arc — seated like metadata. The function works ("the actual function worked — it's the style/layout/path"). The **place** does not exist yet.

The Shelf's breakthrough was not the bookcase; it was **one continuous piece** — the whole library living inside a single thing, every scattered control dissolved into it. The arc's equivalent was already latent in canon: F9 put the wheat field on the shelf's horizon ("the field is where thinking grows, always on the horizon of the library") and RD-1 turned the workshop into a parchment daylight field. The arc interior is the one surface that most deserves to be the field — and wasn't one. This round makes it one.

## §2 — RULINGS (binding)

### F1 · THE PLACE = THE FIELD, WHOLE-HOG
The arc interior is literally the place the wheat horizon has been pointing at. **One continuous ground. No panels floating on it, ever.** Sub-theories are planted *in* the field, not listed over it. The central question lives on the horizon. Controls consolidate at the field's edge (F5). The dark-panel seam ceases to exist by construction: no panel, no seam.

### F2 · THE HOUR = DAYLIGHT
RD-1 extended from the panel to the whole interior. The entire arc surface is a **parchment daylight field** (`--page-2` family ground), one world with the shelf and the workshop. Mark identity reads as **pigment and form, not light** (see F6); attention reads as the gold single-coal grammar already felt-passed on the shelf. Entering the page is walking deeper into the same world — no world flip at the writing door.
**Named revert:** RD-1's DUSK CARVE stays armed. If the mockup's mass-legibility recon fails on light ground, the revert is the fallback — not a crisis, not a redesign.
**Hour reconciliation *(sweep fold-in)*:** since RD-1, the Hour world shipped (v3.232+, AES-2/PG-1: one twilight world, every page a lit sheet floating on it with visible world margins). The field obeys that canon: **the arc interior is one lit sheet on the Hour's twilight** — horizon at its head, soil at its foot, world margins visible. F1's one-ground law governs the sheet's *interior*: no panels ever float on the field itself.

### F3 · THE WRITING SURFACE = THE CLEARING — one place, three distances
Tap a mark and the field **draws close around it**: you arrive at a clearing where that one thing grows — gathered notes standing around the edges (the workshop, exactly as RD-4/RD-5 shaped it), the prose at the center. Lean all the way in and you are at the page: same place, deepest focus, and **the light concentrates** — the page being the app's brightest surface is now depth of attention, not a ground flip. Field → clearing → page is one continuous zoom.
- **R6 intact:** page and workshop remain distinct surfaces — different *distances*, never a merger. All shipped Room behavior preserved.
- **Signature build item:** the transition choreography itself — approach and settle, never route-swap teleport.

### F4 · THE FINISHED ROOM = THE HORIZON COMPLETES
Unanswered, the horizon carries the question alone — open sky, the field growing toward it. On finishing, **the answer joins the question on the horizon**: one line of sky, question and answer standing together as the completed thought. The field's weather turns with it — harvest register, maturity gold fully arrived — so a finished arc reads finished at a glance, badge-free.
- **Values:** when the arc carries values, they appear as quiet ember chips riding the horizon line (the shelf's value-ember grammar). When there are none, **there is nothing.** The "Values this carries" panel dissolves entirely. No furniture in the sky.

### F5 · THE CONTROLS = THE GATE ROW
One slim dock at the field's edge, under the horizon:
- **Name**, edit-in-place, at left — with the FF-7 mono kicker carrying status words. **EMBER is a kicker word, not a button.** Rename ceases to exist as a control.
- **One primary act:** `+ Sub-theory` — the planting gesture.
- **One lifecycle door pill**, rightmost, mirroring the workshop's door dialect (RD-4a) — contents per F8.
- **⋯ overflow**, off-row per F-A: Delete arc, Return to ember (S3R reverse gear), and rare acts.
- **The Field/Read/Page switcher DISSOLVES.** This is the round's one behavior merge, flagged and accepted. One field: drag = arrange, tap = approach (F7). The Page is reached through marks, not tabs. Roaming *is* being there.
Seven scattered controls become: a name, one act, one door, one overflow.

### F6 · MARK IDENTITY = SILHOUETTE LEADS
Identity re-weights from hue-primary to **form-primary** — species told apart by shape first, pigment second.
- **Hue budget *(re-pointed at the sweep)*:** the **five shipped jewel families (m1–m5)** — B2's ruled 16→5 collapse, already live and re-frozen. Not `--field-1..10` (pre-Hour language, superseded).
- **Multipliers:** strong distinct silhouettes × 2–3 treatments (solid / outline / hatched) → well over 16 marks that read unique at field distance.
- **The picker becomes a MARK COMPOSER:** whole composed marks are chosen, form leading — never a hue row.
- **Uniqueness law:** no two marks in one arc may share a full combination. Within-arc uniqueness is guaranteed, not hoped for.
- **The mint unchanged:** identity arrives whole at naming. Unnamed motes and basins remain **formless** — no composed identity before the mint (this now falls out of the grammar automatically).
This resolves the candy-glyph debt at the root: candy was hue doing a job it cannot do; form can.

### F7 · THE CORE GESTURE = TAP = APPROACH (staged)
- **Tap 1 = step closer:** the mark lights, its threads illuminate, its read speaks (the Read face's soul, preserved), and a quiet door appears on the lit mark — "Open →".
- **Tap the lit mark (or its door) = arrive:** the field draws close; you are in the clearing (F3).
- **Tap elsewhere = release.**
- **Drag = arrange**, untouched (RD-5). Press-hold = drag at 390.
- **Positions sacred:** the Approach zoom is a camera move. It never moves a mark, never reflows the arrangement. (Covenant law — see §3.)

### F8 · THE LIFECYCLE DOOR = ONE SEAT, STATE-AWARE
The Gate Row seats one lifecycle door; its label is the arc's honest state (FF-7 destination-named dialect):
- Growing arc: **"Finish →"** — opens the shipped S2 ceremony.
- Finished arc: **"Publish →"**.
- Published arc: quiet **Published** state in the same seat, carrying share access.
One seat, three lives. The S1 disabled-button law holds by construction — no dead Publish button standing while the arc grows. Return to ember lives in the ⋯ overflow, off-row.

### G1 · THE BOOKS = THE SOIL ROW
The arc's member books get a placed seat: a **low shelf-dialect band at the field's foot** — "what this field grows from." Covers standing quietly below the arrangement canvas, in the shelf's cover dialect. Books are ground, not growth: **never inside the arrangement canvas.**

### G2 · THE CLEARING IS SPARSE
No sibling marks at the clearing's edges. The kicker breadcrumb alone carries where-you-are. The mockup may explore a **single faint horizon line** as the only continuity cue — nothing more.

### G3 · MIGRATION = DETERMINISTIC RE-POINT
Existing marks (16-hue identities on real accounts) migrate by deterministic mapping: **hue → nearest budget family; nothing else changes silently.** Recomposing a mark is offered via the composer, never applied — the repair grammar (offered, never applied) extended to identity. ***(Sweep note:** the hue half already shipped — B2's jewel collapse `((N-1) mod 5)+1` is live with stable identities. G3's remaining job is the silhouette/treatment layer: existing marks keep their current forms; the composer offers recomposition.)*

### G4 · GATHER = REACH INTO THE SOIL *(addendum, ruled same day)*
The soil row is present in the clearing — this arc's books at the field's foot. **Tap a book and its passages rise**: the user's marks and annotations from that book offered as candidates; chosen ones stand in the field. The **corpus rail remains the wider door** for everything beyond this arc's books (all reading, searchable — shipped machinery, untouched). The native-select "Pull from reading" dropdown **retires by replacement** — the P-B control-dialect fracture resolved on this surface at the root.

### G5 · WEAVE = DRAG ACROSS THE EDGE *(addendum, ruled same day)*
Dragging a standing note from the field **into the prose weaves it at the drop point** as the anchored cite chip — the fused-notebook gutter gesture, native to the clearing. The full gesture law: **tap = door · drag within the field = arrange · drag across the edge = weave.** Chip↔note lighting both ways stays as shipped. **Accessibility twin:** the note's opened surface (ROOM-2) gains a "Weave into prose" act — keyboard and rail users get an honest path; arrangement stays pointer/touch, weaving does not.

**Concept mapping — no new ruling needed:** the Room charter's law stands (the field *is* the concept map; arranging is drafting; position is a thinking aid, never interpreted; covenant tripwire absolute). Explicit note-to-note connection grammar belongs to R10 — named seam, not this round.

### G6 · THE CANVAS = FULL `createWritingCanvas` ADOPTION *(addendum, ruled same day)*
The clearing and page builds adopt **`createWritingCanvas`** (the writing-core substrate — Lifted Sheet treatment, Cormorant canvas) as the prose surface: a real bounded writing surface with honest focus states, replacing the legacy raw textarea. This **closes the sub-theory "lone split-pane holdout"** — the central 2.0 writing-surfaces unification debt — inside this round rather than deferring the migration. Every other input on arc surfaces (name field, values entry, gather controls) speaks the R-POLISH kit dialect, censused at recon. **Named fallback** if recon finds the shipped cite-weave closure too entangled with the substrate swap: canvas on the page, dressed textarea in the clearing, remainder as named debt — never silent.

### SWEEP FOLD-INS *(ruled July 22, batch-confirmed)*
- **Motion rides MO-1:** the approach zoom uses the shipped motion canon (~300ms glide, existing easings) — no invented durations. Under reduced-motion, the zoom is an **instant crossfade**; continuity is carried by the breadcrumb and horizon, not by animation.
- **The sequential footer retires:** the "next sub-theory ›" footer nav imposes a reading order the arc's oldest constraint forbids. The field is the navigator — one honest gesture back (the D3 return rider), then approach the next mark yourself. Retired by ruling, not silently.
- **The pre-question horizon:** a new arc before its question is authored shows open sky and one quiet invitation line in the never-asked-never-forbidden dialect. The horizon obeys sparse-honest from birth.

## §3 — LAWS (felt canon — binding acceptance criteria)

Each sentence below is an acceptance criterion for the mockup and the build. A break returns to chat.

1. **One ground.** The arc interior is one continuous parchment field — itself a lit sheet on the Hour's twilight, world margins visible. Nothing floats on the field as a panel; the law governs the sheet's interior. (F1/F2)
2. **One world.** Field, clearing, and page are one place at three distances; brightness = depth of attention; no ground flip anywhere on the path. (F3)
3. **The horizon speaks.** The question stands on the horizon at every distance's edge; on finishing, the answer joins it — and nothing else ever rides the sky except value embers when they exist. (F4)
4. **Sparse-honest.** Empty carries no furniture: an ember arc is open ground, the question on the horizon, and at most one `--ink-3` line. An empty values state is nothing. (F4, RD-3 dialect)
5. **One gate.** All arc chrome lives in the Gate Row: name + kicker, one act, one door, one overflow. No control ever floats elsewhere on the field. (F5)
6. **Form first.** A mark is told apart by silhouette before hue; no two marks in an arc share a full identity combination; nothing has composed identity before the mint. (F6)
7. **Approach, never teleport.** Every tap moves one distance deeper; the door is always one honest tap away and never an accidental one; the camera moves, the arrangement never does. (F7)
8. **Honest doors.** The lifecycle seat shows only the act that can be taken, named for its destination. (F8)
9. **Soil below, sky above.** Books stand at the field's foot in the shelf dialect; the question stands in the sky; the growth between them belongs to the user's hand alone. (G1 + covenant)
10. **Threads are gold.** All connection chrome speaks the gold-ink family (F6 cyan→gold completed); flow is subtle, CSS-only, and still under reduced-motion. (fold-in)
11. **The pull is physical.** Sources rise from the soil into the field, and pass from the field into the prose by the hand — tap raises, drag weaves; no dropdown ever stands between a book and the argument it feeds. (G4/G5)
12. **Writing has a body.** Prose lives on a real canvas — bounded, lifted, with honest focus — never a bare textarea floating on the ground; every control on these surfaces speaks the kit dialect. (G6)

**Covenant tripwire (carried, absolute):** the user authors the arrangement; the app composes defaults and offers tidying, never auto-reflows; position is expressive spatial memory, never load-bearing for meaning and never interpreted. `evidenceLayout`-class data never enters any context assembly.

- **The Hour world (AES-2/PG-1, shipped):** one twilight world; lit sheets where you work; depth is meaning. The field composes as a sheet under this canon; jewel tokens m1–m5 are the mark hue base.
- **MO-1 motion canon (shipped):** two durations (~150/~300ms), two easings, reduced-motion global — the approach choreography draws from this vocabulary only.
- **Two horizons law (new fold-in):** the shelf's horizon is the wheat — the field seen from outside the library's window. The arc's horizon is **open sky carrying the question** — no wheat art under display type, no text-over-art collision. On the shelf you see the field; in the arc you are in it.
- **Ground check / ES3 / Universal tokens / reduced-motion stillness:** all standing.

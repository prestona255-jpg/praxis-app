## 2 · THE LAWS (all ruled July 18, tappable, at rec)

**PG-1 · THE PAGE-GROUND LAW — "paper by day, sky for beholding."** Working/reading surfaces are full-bleed light paper: Home joins its siblings and loses the boxed-sheet-in-dark frame (its warmth survives IN the paper via the gradient family, not via a dark vignette). Dark is reserved for beholding surfaces only — the Profile galaxy and the About hero — and every dark→light crossing is a ruled soft seam (a dawn, not a cut; Profile's hard ~630px cut is the named offender). The arc Field converts to parchment per RD-1.

**C-PATTERNS · THE CONTROL CANON (CC-1…CC-4).**
- **CC-1 selects:** custom listbox ≥760 (house dialect, keyboard + focus complete); styled native <760 — the native picker IS the premium touch feel. P3 (44px) / P7 (font-16) intact.
- **CC-2 field ground:** all inputs/selects/textareas use the CARVED/inset grammar — controls are carved INTO the paper (one inset shadow, ground-layer recess), content sits ON it. L2 speaks L3's dialect.
- **CC-3 role law:** chips = state/filter/selection and toggle; buttons = verbs and fire. ONE primary-gold action per view (restraint-law echo). The off-palette teal-gold "+ Add a sub-theory" converts to canon gold.
- **CC-4 states law:** focus-visible = 2px gold ring (--gold on dark grounds, --gold-deep on light; extends D6) · hover = ground shifts one surface step · disabled = 40% ink, no pointer · ONE error treatment · **destructive = quiet, cornered, confirm-gated** — re-places DELETE ARC (arc header) and "Delete this sub-theory" (floating mid-canvas).
- Control set the kit must cover (from census): listbox/select · text input · search input · textarea · chip (state/filter/tab) · segmented control (ONE style; four exist today) · button (primary/secondary/quiet) · toggle switch · dashed affordance ("+ mark a value" family) · destructive pattern.

**GR-1 · THE ONE ARC GRAMMAR — the living miniature.** An arc looks like itself everywhere: thumbnails on Home and the Arcs index render that arc's ACTUAL field arrangement — the user's real placements — as gold-channel marks on parchment with --field-1..10 hues (RD-1 grammar). Candy clusters and constellation dot-lines both retire. One grammar from thumbnail to interior; sparse arcs get the same composed-sparse treatment as the field itself (P-F clause), so an empty thumbnail still reads designed.

**XL-1 · THE COMPOSITION LAW — the desk widens (tier ≥1600, designed at 1920, stretch-verified 2560).** Pages compose wider by archetype, never stretch: list pages (Shelf*, Arcs index, arc Read face) gain columns/density · detail pages (Book Detail, sub-theory page) gain a wider ≤72ch measure plus a persistent right rail that actually carries content (sub-theory's rail = its existing values/connections/sources cards) · spread pages (Notebook) widen both leaves · the Field spans its working region beside its rail as a bounded desk (**RM6 resolved as XL-1 derivative**; exact pixel cap = B2 recon). All governed by the ≥60% occupancy floor restated at XL. *Shelf = exemption, see §4.

**DR-1 · THE DRAG LAW.** Pick-up: scale ~1.03, shadow deepens, origin slot dims. Move: valid targets brighten on approach. Settle: slight-overshoot ease into place, shadow collapses. Reduced-motion: instant swap, no choreography. Applies to the Field, the workshop field, and any future drag surface.

**NBK-1 · THE LEAF KEEPS GROWING, COMPOSED (RECONCILED 2026-07-19, B1 — the guard fired).** ~~The Notebook's note stream scrolls WITHIN the leaf so the two-leaf spread composes at any length.~~ The guard clause below was invoked and the original premise is **withdrawn**: Slice-0 recon found the growing leaf was ruled **deliberately**, across three independent artifacts (the source mockups `design/notebook.html` + `praxis-the-notebook-amber.html`, min-height with no scroll · the ship checkpoint `notebook-hybrid.md:26` · DW-2's 200-note stress test, which measured render time on a page that grows). A bounded/scrolling leaf would be a NEW design decision, not a polish fix, so B1 **honours the growth and composes it**: the spread's frame grows gracefully with its stream, and the *other* leaf — the real defect, a tall blank column beside a long stream — gets the SYS-1 composed-empty treatment. GUARD (as written, and satisfied): B1 recon first checks whether the shipped bound-notebook fidelity ruled the growing leaf deliberately; if so, surface that ruling and reconcile — never silently override.

**AMB-1 · THE CORNER LAW (amended by R-CAPTURE CD-1).** Two ruled fixed elements, each meaningful: bottom-left = the "+" create door (R-CAPTURE's), bottom-right = the Yumi flower — one ruled size and glow each. Nothing else ever floats. The ⓘ retires; its orientation content lives in About/nav. No captions anywhere (L5: RD-6's chip is the affordance).

**TY-1 · THE TYPE SYSTEM.** A ruled 5-role ramp: display / title / heading / body / meta. Cormorant floored ≥~24px — below the floor, DM Sans medium takes titles. DM Mono demoted to TRUE meta only (timestamps, counts, ISBN, technical labels); section headers move up the ramp into real hierarchy. **THE READER'S-WORDS LAW:** user marginalia gets the finest setting in the app — its own register, distinct from system italic, and UPRIGHT at length (italics slow readers and punish dyslexic ones; italics are reserved for accents) — so the reader's words outrank the chrome everywhere they appear. The ramp carries a 12px absolute floor; nothing in the app renders smaller. Microcopy register rule rides here: the poetic voice and the instrument voice each get a ruled role; no mixing within a surface.

**MO-1 · THE MOTION CANON.** Two durations (fast ~150ms · gentle ~300ms), two easings, six named moments applied app-wide: hover lift · press · selection · panel reveal · route cross-fade · SAVE PULSE (every capture/save visibly answers the hand). Reduced-motion kills all of it globally. DR-1's drag belongs to this system, not performing alone in a static app.

**SYS-1 · THE FOUNDATION KIT (Slice 0 expansion).** Beyond the control kit: a --space token scale + per-tier grid (the metric layer XL-1 composes on — without it, per-batch passes re-invent paddings and drift regrows) · **ICON LAW** — one stroke family, one size grid, no emoji or mixed glyph dialects in chrome · **EMPTY + LOADING STATE LAW** — every empty state teaches or invites (Home's lower half is an empty state; treat it like one), one ruled loading treatment for Yumi/network latency · **TRUNCATION RULE** for long titles at every tier · **AUTOMATED CONTRAST GATE** in the census — every token pair machine-checked ≥4.5:1, failures filed as findings. Cost accepted: Slice 0 ≈ two sessions.

**UX-1 · THE LEXICON-IN-PLACE LAW.** Every term of art (arc, sub-theory, marginalia, weave, gather, alight, EMBER, NASCENT, Graduate, resonance, faint, incorporated, lenses, values…) becomes a quiet affordance where it appears: tap/hover → a one-line plain meaning + a path to the About lexicon. One definition component in the kit; applied per batch. SEAM: the onboarding round owns the first-run teaching sequence; this law owns the standing surfaces — no double-own.

**UX-2 · THE WAYFINDING LAW.** One location grammar app-wide: a ruled breadcrumb pattern (Arc › Sub-theory · state) at readable ramp size on every interior surface · Book Detail gains its path back to containing arcs · nav active-state made unmistakable (absorbs the B1 clause) · search labeled for its true scope (Slice 0 recon verifies what it actually searches) · and the About pipeline (book → margin → reflection → praxis) echoed as the chrome's quiet spine, so every surface answers *where am I* and *where does this lead*.

**UX-3 · THE FORGIVENESS LAW.** Destructive actions execute with a ~7s Undo toast (soft-delete buffer) rather than relying on confirms; "cannot be undone" becomes rare, justified, and honestly stated (Reset placements, DELETE ARC, delete sub-theory are the named offenders). All submit actions debounce — killing the double-fire class at the root. One ruled pending/failed-write indicator gives people offline data trust. Cost accepted: touches the state layer; B2/B3 grow slightly.

**ME-1 · THE MEASUREMENT COVENANT.** Privacy-first minimal instrumentation, disclosed plainly: client error capture + anonymous AGGREGATE counts only — no per-user behavioral profiles, no third-party trackers, no session replay, ever. "We count, we never watch," written into About as a covenant section beside the privacy covenant. ACTIVATION defined as a number so onboarding is testable: activated = ≥1 book added + ≥1 marginalia captured in the first session. RETENTION PHILOSOPHY named: the return hook is the reading practice itself — no streaks, no dark patterns, no engagement mechanics; Home's "pick it back up?" is the ceiling. Implementation: the telemetry module + About covenant text ride B4 (About's batch); activation counters land with it.

**CO-1 · THE YUMI COST LAW (SCAN's discipline goes app-wide).** Every generative surface — lenses, values-noticing, arc voice, enrichment, capture assists — obeys the ruled set: known token cost per action · per-user daily budget · honest refusal state when capped · NO silent model degradation, ever · responses stored once and never re-billed on re-render. Slice 0 recon verifies no surface re-calls the API on render. One enthusiastic beta tester is a delight, not an invoice.

**LA-1 · LAUNCH INTEGRITY (a sequence amendment, codified at close-out).** Six scorable items join the existing beta-readiness gate in sequence.md: account deletion works end-to-end (the Account dead routes become a launch blocker, not an exemption) · privacy policy + terms page · Lighthouse budget (perf ≥90, a11y ≥95) + WCAG 2.1 AA as the named conformance target · scheduled Firestore backup WITH one tested restore · iOS PWA capture-sync verification (Safari storage eviction + local-first capture = a data-loss vector until proven synced) · first-session observation protocol for the first 3–5 beta users, filed as a named artifact of the beta loop. Named for later: the GR-1 living miniature becomes each published arc's OG share image when Stage-12/commons work fires.

**Clause-level rulings (no forks needed):** SEMANTIC GOLD — gold means presence and attention, never decoration; one sentence added to the restraint laws. ONE DAYLIGHT GRADIENT recipe under PG-1 (Home's lamplight, Shelf's flat, Field's radial converge). ## 6 · FELT CANON (the acceptance sentences — each verdict is one of these, felt on the deployed build)
- **XL:** At 1920 no page reads as a column floating in a void; everything composes, nothing you need lives off-frame.
- **Controls:** Every control is recognizably Praxis — carved into the paper, one dialect; no OS chrome appears on desktop.
- **Grammar:** An arc looks like itself everywhere — the thumbnail is the field you arranged, and you recognize yours at a glance.
- **Drag:** Picking up a mark feels like lifting a real thing; setting it down feels like it lands.
- **Ground:** The app is one paper world by day; dark appears only where you behold, and every crossing is a dawn, not a cut.
- **Presence:** The flower is the only quiet thing in the corner; nothing captions her.
- **Type:** The reader's own words are the most beautiful text on every page, and every header speaks at its true size.
- **Motion:** The app answers your hand — every press, save, and crossing responds; nothing snaps.
- **Understanding:** A stranger can read any page and learn its words without leaving it, and always knows where they are and where a thing leads.
- **Forgiveness:** Nothing in Praxis is one reflexive click from permanent loss, and your data tells you the truth about itself — online or off.
- **Stewardship:** The app counts but never watches; every cost, limit, and refusal tells the truth; nothing about the product's health is a mystery.

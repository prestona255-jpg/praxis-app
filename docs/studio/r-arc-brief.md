## 4b. THE PEDAGOGY + EPISTEMOLOGY LAYER (walked July 14 — equally binding; Q1/Q2 as amended)

**At the shaping moment (S1):**
- **Q5 AUTHORSHIP = notice-and-ask.** The raw eruption IS the artifact. Yumi's shaping is an overlay of anchored notices + questions ("Did you mean Gholdy Muhammad?"); repairs offered, never applied; **nothing enters the seedling the reader didn't tap.** ⭐ **NEW COVENANT LINE: "Yumi never speaks for the reader."**
- **Q6 SOURCES = books-spine + threads.** Books stay the canon's spine. Thinkers and concepts live as lightweight **threads** on the seedling, graduating into books when a text is chosen. Thread-resolution help = bibliographic pointing via the opt-in web layer, angle-style.
- **Q7-ROUTING = notice-and-ask.** Kinship surfaced as a notice; the reader taps: new seedling · fold into the existing arc · new-with-kinship-thread. Never routed silently.

**The seedling's life (S2):**
- **Q7-LIFECYCLE = earned-then-declared.** Graduation is always the reader's act, *invited* when the seedling shows life (≥1 candidate source read + marginalia'd AND ≥1 seed minted). **Seedlings never auto-delete** — they dim; compost is manual and recoverable.
- **Q11 READING LENS = active, staged, sheddable.** Candidate books carry the seedling's mark + question as presence; one optional tap stitches note→seedling; Yumi's book-probes may be angled by the question (preamble seam). **LAW: the lens is always sheddable.** Deep move: "is the question too small?"

**Between seed and sub-theory (Preston's amendment — late naming):**
- **The seed's four lives.** (1) *Noticed:* a seed enters carrying only its **origin phrase**, verbatim — no title field exists. (2) *Gathering:* the seed is an open **basin** accreting fragments, insights, stitched notes, and pulls — unordered, no prose demanded; basins can **split** and **merge**. (3) *The naming threshold:* at real mass, naming becomes invited — "this gathering keeps circling something — what would you call it?" (4) **The name is the mint:** naming is the transformative act — the seed becomes a sub-theory, takes its mark, and the workshop opens with the basin pre-loaded in the rail.
- **Naming gate = never asked, never forbidden.** No title field until invited; a deliberate act can still name early. Invitation over enforcement — graduation's grammar, applied consistently.
- **VISUAL LAW: form follows naming.** Named sub-theories have shaped marks; unnamed seeds are **formless motes** whose only visual property is brightness (= accreted mass). The field shows thinking at different temperatures.

**In the room (S3):**
- **Q1 FIRST MOVE (AMENDED) = seam-first, delivered as the first raised hand.** Her opening move — a tension between two of the reader's own passages, as a question — arrives **pre-lit** when the reader enters, and opens on tap. **Never-moves:** never summarizes the pile · never proposes thesis language · never fills gaps with content · never grades the draft · never repairs prose. **Silences:** while text flows · while the draft is too young · right after a weave · when dismissed (sticky per session).
- **Q2 SPEAKING MODEL (AMENDED) = the raised hand, absolute.** **Yumi never speaks unbidden, ever** — no exceptions. She signals: a quiet anchored indicator; her notice unfolds only on tap. Unread hands age quietly.
- **Q3 CRITIQUE LINE = structural grounding probes.** Observable weave-state only (claim on 0–1 chips · woven-but-unengaged · chips in tension · source in prose absent from weave); always observation + question, never verdict; auditable by construction. **New move family — own eval battery.**
- **Q9 RETRIEVAL = legible, deterministic-first.** The rail generates the candidate set; Yumi selects from it; every pull carries its visible *why*; the passed-over set is one tap away; rail vs Yumi visually distinct. No asymmetric knowledge.
- **Q10 PRIVACY = two visibilities + the Finish gate.** The rail serving the reader sees everything they own; Yumi's set respects her consent flags absolutely. Provenance rides every chip; Finish runs a per-chip gate: publish / paraphrase-out / hold.

**At Finish:**
- **Q-B EARNING = grounded + made + answering.** One reflective-threshold question — "How does this answer to [the central question]?" — non-blocking; if answered, captured as the sub-theory's **answering line**, visible per mark on the arc. S4 inherits its material for free.
- **CHOREOGRAPHY LAW (critique):** deterministic privacy sweep first (only if private chips exist) → the threshold question as **one quiet full-screen moment — the question is the door** to the amber room. Never stacked modals.

**At every depth:**
- **Q-C = same grammar.** Yumi does not change with corpus depth. Every corpus-dependent move declares a floor and falls back one rung (seam → draw-out → silence), never fabricates. Guide-mode rejected: the restraint IS the pedagogy.

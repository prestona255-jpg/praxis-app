## 1. The ruled ontology

**A value is:** a named commitment + a **living statement** (the reader's own theorized definition — one revisable articulation in their voice, revision itself visible as growth) + an **evidence trail** (marks with why-lines; subs drawing on it).

**A connection is:** a **work↔work relation standing on a theorized ground.** Values are grounds, never nodes — a value emits zero lines and is never an endpoint. Switching a value on reveals its ground.

**Two tiers:**

- **Co-presence (derived, quiet).** Works whose marks place them on the same ground simply *gather* there. No pairwise lines are ever drawn between them. Rationale: a mark on work A is the reader's writing *about A*; a derived line between A and B is a relation nobody wrote — co-occurrence in the evidence rule's clothes. Co-presence also kills the last jumble structurally: N works on a ground render as N points, never N².
- **Threads (ratified, named).** A line exists **only** when the reader ratifies it. The reader's yes — ideally with a written why — is the writing the relation stands on.

**Scope ruling (CN-6):** R10 covers **value-grounded connections only**. Cross-arc citations and sub-theory echoes are real connection kinds but are **named future types**, not this round's ontology — citations already have a shipped surface (the lineage row), and folding them in would put two ontologies in one round.

**Thread ground (CN-7):** one ground per thread in v1. Works may appear on many grounds; the thread object stands on exactly one. Multi-ground (intersectional) threads = a **named open question** at the round open — the intersectional principle gets an explicit slot, not silence.

**Presets reframed:** the 10 preset values are *invitations*; the living statement is the *appropriation*. You may adopt the word "Care," but you must make it yours. (Critical-pedagogy grounding: received value categories are banked concepts; a value you define, revise, and ground in your own reading is praxis.)

## 2. The curation function — what makes a line worth drawing

**A line is worth drawing when the reader said yes to it.** Full pipeline:

1. Co-presence accumulates automatically from marks (evidence-rule-clean, zero effort).
2. The reader notices a relation on the ground page, **or** Yumi poses one (draw-out register, index-grounded to the reader's own marks, quoting their own why-lines back as questions).
3. The reader ratifies → names the thread → (invited, optional) writes its why → the line exists.

**Evidence rule, extended for R10:** marks justify a work's *presence on a ground*; only ratification justifies a *relation*. Nothing draws without a yes.

## 3. Posture and placement laws

- **Central in meaning, quiet in chrome.** R10 converges the existing ~8 value touchpoints (onboarding beat, Account stones, marks, shelf rail, retrofit, profile statement/lighting/lineage) into one legible system — the touchpoints become *doors to ground pages*. No ninth ambient sprinkle.
- **Write-silent law.** Composition surfaces carry zero value chrome. Marking is a deliberate act at reflective moments (post-publish, review, distill posture) — the mark stays weighty precisely because it is not ambient. *Consequence:* R8 placed mark registers on sub-theory and arc surfaces (composition-adjacent); honoring write-silent likely relocates those. Book detail (a reflective surface) keeps its register. Vehicle = R10 rider or S-B item — round open decides.

## 5. The interaction contract (CN-2)

A ratified thread supports two verbs, ruled here:
- **WALK** — a sequenced read through the thread's works, their why-lines, under the value's definition.
- **WRITE-FROM** — spawn a sub-theory or journal entry from the thread.

Tap a thread → its walk. Tap a co-present work → the work itself. **PUBLISH a thread** (traveling with the portrait / discoverable in the commons) = **HANDOFF to the social-discovery brief** per the seam rules.

A connection that can't be acted on is decoration; threads are the one form of connection that can't be.

## 6. Yumi — two ruled roles, both eval-gated

- **Definition layer:** tension-posing between the stated definition and the accumulated evidence ("your Care definition says X; your marks on Y pull elsewhere"). Never drafts a definition — a theorized value is only pedagogic if the theorizing is the reader's.
- **Line layer:** proposes candidate threads from co-presence; the reader ratifies or dismisses. Nothing draws without a yes.

**Laws carried in:** RF1/LENS-1 index-grounding governs any full-library pass (output-token truncation lesson); honest empty vs. error states (found-nothing ≠ call-failed); proposals cite the reader's own marks/why-lines only.

## 12. Anti-patterns — what Connections will never be

- An auto-generated **similarity graph** (no derived relation lines, ever — CN-5 is law).
- A **recommendation engine**: Yumi never suggests acquisitions, never uses cross-reader similarity; proposals cite only the reader's own writing.
- A **completeness score or tally** (evidence-weighted law holds; no counts-as-meaning).
- **Ambient value chrome** on composition surfaces (write-silent law).
- Yumi **drafting definitions** or issuing verdicts.
- A generalized-vocabulary system: no line stands on an untheorized slug.

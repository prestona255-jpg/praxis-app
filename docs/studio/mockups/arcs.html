The Pedagogy of Refusal

bright

Refusal is not withdrawal — it is the first shape attention takes when it decides what it will not do.

Read against Foucault's disciplinary apparatus and Scott's "weapons of the weak," refusal here is reframed not as passivity but as a counter-discipline — a patterned withholding that produces its own kind of knowledge.

Planet hue wheel — deterministic per slug, AA on night ground

One muted warm-family wheel; each category (and each lens, in lens mode) hashes to a fixed hue, so a field keeps its colour across sessions and across the category/lens switch. Lines, stars and specks stay gold-family so they read over any hue. Contrast is measured against the night ground #1c1209 — planet fills are translucent graphics (AA-large ≥3:1 is the bar), but every hue is printed with its full ratio.

Felt-pass decisions this mockup asks for

  1. Core silhouette — hexagon (wisdom tradition-form), compass (four-point star), or spark (six-point star)? Toggle “Core”.

  3. Motion feel — drift amplitude + ring/orbit speeds (planets drift 34–41s, rings 64–150s, orbits ≥120s, breath 6.5s). Faster/slower/stiller?

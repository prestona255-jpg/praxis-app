The sunflower, v1.2

Ochre page with a shade more range, one yellow to press, a brown sky at two depths with its light spilling onto the paper, a stem green between the stars, terracotta for the one thing on the screen that is alive. The paper has tooth; the sky is smooth.

What v7 settles

  1. The door. One pressed gold circle, 56px, bottom-right, above the tab bar on phone and at the window's corner on desktop. Every surface. The paper keeps 88px clear beneath it so nothing is ever covered.
  2. Yumi's seed replaces the smile. Glyph only, her seat is the sky color.
  3. The rail shows covers, as the Shelf does. No gold stripe. A missing cover is a third-step block with the title.
  4. Italic Fraunces belongs to anything with a voice: Yumi's lines and named arcs. The app's own words stay roman.
  5. Type scale. Greeting 46 (66 desktop) at weight 500, counts 34, labels 11 mono tracked wide (the HIG floor).
  6. Light. The lamp spills 140px onto the paper; stars glow; every card catches a hairline on its top edge.
  7. Grain at .32 multiply by day, .22 screen by night, paper only.
  8. Still. No ambient motion in the sky. Day sky stays #2B1E12; Yumi's dock stays at the foot.

Every text/ground pair measured in both moods; lowest is 4.53 (gold-ink on the page). Cover art here is typographic placeholder, not the books' real jackets.

The same home at 1360

Composed, not stretched. The sky becomes a column that stays put while the paper scrolls; the door sits at the window's corner. The mood switch flips this frame too.

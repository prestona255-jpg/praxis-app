Praxis · design canon

A warm study, at night

The craft that leads every Praxis surface. A reading portrait rendered like an observatory: warm paper for the room you sit in, a deep cool-warm night for the galaxy of your thinking. This document is the reference — palette, type, the galaxy’s laws, motion, components, voice, and the line between what’s public and what stays yours.

01

Philosophy

Praxis is a place to build theory from reading. The profile is not a dashboard and not a feed — it is a portrait of a mind: the values someone reads toward, and the thinking those values have grown into.

The room is warm. The sky is deep. Your thinking is the light.

Three commitments hold the whole system together:

The portrait teaches how you read, the interior stays yours. Everything public is legible enough to show a stranger how a mind works; everything intimate — the margins, the drafts, the unfinished — stays with the reader. This line (§09) is load-bearing, not a setting.

Poetry lives in the content, precision lives in the chrome. Titles, excerpts, margin notes, and value articulations can sing. Labels, counts, and controls stay plainspoken and exact. The tension between a grand serif and a whisper-small mono label is the house style.

An instrument, not a mirror. The portrait never concludes who the reader is — a profile that pronounces identity is the banking model wearing a nice suit. It offers, asks, and lets the reader play it: categorization is a dialogue (offer → confirm, rename, or reject), gaps and tensions render as questions, and the emblem asks “does this look like you?” rather than asserting it. This principle is already live on the account-page portrait and every future surface inherits it.

One deliberate risk, everything else quiet. The signature is the galaxy, and specifically its warm-core-to-cool-edge night — a warm study that opens onto a cold sky. The warm-paper-and-serif shell is Praxis’s established brand, kept consistent on purpose; the cool depth in the galaxy is the one place we spend boldness.

Gilding is how the room gets its richness without losing quiet: a 1px gilded hairline across the top of cards (inset 18px each side, fading to nothing), a gradient gold fill with an inner top light on primary buttons, a faint gold-hi glow pooled behind the values statement like lamplight, metric numbers ink-to-gold at their feet, and a hairline gold ring offset around the avatar. Gilding is an edge treatment and a glow — never a fill on large surfaces.

Gold is the accent, but gold-deep is what you use for gold-colored text or icons on cream — plain --gold fails contrast at body sizes. Ember is reserved strictly for destructive or error states; it never decorates.

Typography

Three voices. Cormorant Garamond is the soul — every title, number, value, and piece of writing. DM Sans is the speaking voice — body and interface. DM Mono is the instrument panel — labels, counts, and meta, always small and tracked.

Editorial rules

separators
Values divide with a 1.5px gold hairline at 45% opacity — never a slash or bullet.
accents
Emphasis inside a serif line is italic + gold, not bold. Bold is for DM Sans / DM Mono only.
drop cap
Long excerpts open with a two-line initial-letter drop cap in gold-deep.
case
Sentence case everywhere except mono kicker labels, which are uppercase.
numbers
All counts and metrics are Cormorant, large and confident — numbers are part of the portrait.

Space & form

Generous, editorial rhythm. The page breathes; density lives only inside the galaxy.

column
1000–1010px max content width, centered.
section rhythm
52–56px between major sections; a hairline rule separates reference sections.
radius
16px cards & panels · 12–13px tiles & wells · 999px chips, pills, buttons.
card padding
22–26px for content cards; 15–18px for tiles.
grids
auto-fit minmax for tiles/cards so surfaces reflow to one column on mobile without bespoke breakpoints.
elevation
Cards lift on hover: translateY(-2px) + a soft warm shadow. The night panel carries an inner top edge-light so it reads as a window.
gilding
Content cards carry the 1px gilded hairline at their top edge (§02). Primary buttons take the gilded fill. One glow pools behind the hero.
vignette
The page has a faint warm radial glow at the top — the sense of a lamp in the room.
05

The galaxy

The signature. A portrait of a mind as an observatory field. Read it top-down: fields are where you read, books are the ground, sub-theories are the light your thinking makes, and values are the lines that run between fields.

Planet = field
A category or lens. Size = books read there. The territory of your reading.
Bright star = sub-theory
A piece of your thinking, near its home field. Size = books behind it. Glints. Opens its page.
Faint star = book
One of the books you’ve read — the ground the thinking rises from. Ambient, many, dim.
Thread = value
A value that recurs across fields. Radiates from the field carrying it most.

Encoding laws

planet size
r = 17 + booksRead × 0.42 — the more you’ve read in a field, the larger its world.
star size
Sub-theory: r = 3.3 + booksBehind × 0.85. A sub-theory built on more reading burns bigger.
luminosity
A book’s brightness encodes engagement; sub-theory stars are always bright (they’re the heroes) and carry glints.
star placement
A sub-theory sits by its home field — the field of its highest-engagement source book — fanned by golden angle so clusters don’t collide.
a thread forms
When a value has evidence in two or more fields — ≥1 sub-theory/arc drawing on that field, or ≥2 annotated books in it.
constellation
A value radiates from its hub (the field carrying it most) to each other carrying field — n−1 clean lines, never a tangle of pairs.

The three views

Categories
Planets are your reading categories. The default.
Lenses
The same books regroup around your lenses — the interpretive stances you read with.
Numbers
The galaxy recedes; the quantitative half of the portrait takes its place, in the same night. Gold figures on the dark.
06

Motion

Motion is rare and meaningful. One orchestrated moment — a value assembling — carries more than scattered effects. Everything below is gated by prefers-reduced-motion.

constellation draw-in
The signature. On value-select, each thread draws itself from the hub outward — stroke-dashoffset, .6s ease. Watch a value assemble across the sky.
dim & brighten
Selecting a value fades non-members and lifts members — opacity, .35s. Nothing snaps.
hover peek
A star’s core swells ×1.25–1.35; the whisper fades in over .14s.
twinkle
~15% of the faint book-field breathes slowly — 2.5–6.5s, opacity .14↔.55. Life, not sparkle.
card lift
Interactive cards rise 2px with a warm shadow on hover — .2s.
page turn
Navigating to a book or sub-theory fades the destination up .35s; back restores the portrait at its prior scroll.

08

Interaction laws

Two rules govern every surface. Learn them once, they hold everywhere.

hover = peek
Hovering a star shows a whisper — a glance, no commitment.
click = go
Clicking a leaf (a book, a sub-theory) navigates to its page.
structure opens a panel
Clicking a structural node (a field/planet, a value) opens the detail panel in place — it does not navigate.
the graph is walkable
Field → sub-theory → its books → each book’s margins → back — every node links to its neighbors.
trace & release
A value chip toggles its constellation. Tapping empty sky or the chip again clears.
back is faithful
Returning restores the portrait exactly where it was left.
addressable
In the live app every node is a URL — books, sub-theories, arcs, and a traced value are all linkable.
09

Public & private — the two faces

The profile has two readers: the visitor, who sees the portrait, and you, who sees the portrait and its intimate interior. This line is the heart of the design, not a preference.

Public — the portrait

  • Identity, values statement, the prose line
  • The galaxy: fields, the book-field, sub-theory stars, value threads
  • Knowledge arcs and their central questions
  • Published sub-theories & their excerpts
  • Counts, cornerstone books, lineage

Private — stays yours

  • Every note in the margins
  • Drafts and unpublished sub-theories
  • In-progress reads and their progress
  • Per-book engagement and passages saved
  • The reading journal

How the interior shows

In the owner view, private items appear inline, marked plainly — a small only you can see this tag, never a wall or a warning. The visitor view simply omits them; it never shows a locked placeholder that advertises what’s hidden.

The consented door. A private margin note can become published evidence only through an explicit, loud make this public step — and the note is visibly re-marked afterward. Nothing private ever becomes public silently. First publish warns, plainly: anyone on the internet can read this.

10

Voice

Plain nouns in the chrome; poetry only in the content. The interface names things by what they are; the writing is where a mind shows.

Do

Label with plain nouns: Books · Knowledge arcs · Sub-theories · Fields · Published.

Name a value with specificity: truth under pressure, not “truth.”

Let excerpts, margin notes, and value articulations carry the poetry.

Write empty states as invitations: your first sub-theory will appear here.

Don’t

Dress labels in metaphor: “carried by,” “expressed in,” “the thinking it grows.”

Flatten a value to a generic word that could mean anything.

Make the chrome poetic — a button says what it does.

Apologize in errors, or leave an empty screen as mood.

When Yumi speaks, the register is a fellow reader — warm, brief, present: “I ask questions while you read. No summaries, just thinking together.” Never a mascot, never a cheerleader.

11

Forming states

A portrait begins empty. The forming states are not error screens — they are the first chapter, and they should feel like a beginning worth having.

new galaxy
No stars yet. The field shows faintly, a nebula waiting. One line: “Your portrait begins here.” The first read lights the first faint star.
no sub-theories
The bright-star layer is empty with a gentle marker: “Your first sub-theory will rise here as a star.”
no values named
The values statement is a prompt, not a blank: an invitation to name what you read toward — Yumi helps if asked.
sparse reading
Few books — the field is quiet and honest. The portrait grows visibly as reading accrues; that growth is the reward.
